# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in bemora a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming language, respecting viewpoints, gracefully accepting criticism, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainers via the contact listed on the repository's GitHub profile, or via [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Demon-radio/Bemora.lol/security/advisories/new) for sensitive matters. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
